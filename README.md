**Problem Statement:**
Farmers often apply fertilizers excessively or at the wrong times, leading to nutrient imbalances in the soil. Therefore, the core objective is to develop solutions that address the negative effects of improper fertilizer use.

**Core Problem with improper fertile usage : **
Soil Degradation: Soil becomes less fertile over time, losing microorganisms and beneficial nutrients.
Lower Crop Yields: Crops may not grow as robustly, leading to reduced yields and profitability.
Economic Strain on Farmers: Unnecessary fertilizer purchases increase costs, while poor yields decrease income

**Importance of sustainable fertilizer use:**
Healthy soil and optimal fertilizer use mean higher crop production and improved income for farmers.
Proper nutrient balance ensures the land remains fertile for generations to come.
Higher yields and stable agriculture support the country’s food supply and keep food prices in check.
